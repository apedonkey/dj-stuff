## The LiDAR hint, reconciled
- The 30 m ring on the plateau field at 272-276 m is the LABYRINTH, not the Stone Circle. Its concentric mown rings are
  visible in leaf-off Esri imagery at 39.7497, -78.3681 (observed; imagery_grid_B). The labyrinth is 76 ft (newer page)
  / 84 ft (older page) across = 23-26 m, with 4 tall solstice poles on its rim and granite curb stones; the mascot
  balloon sat in its centre at Big Dub 2026 (fly-over t=63-84 s). The Stone Circle is a 200 ft (61 m) ellipse under
  full forest canopy and is not visible from above.
- The "big central field climbing 242-256 m" plus the "plateau field 272-276 m" are one pasture: the Hilltop field,
  rising north from the farmhouse (755 ft / 230 m) to ~890 ft at its north end. 4qf: "pasture runs uphill from the
  farmhouse to the hilltop; the labyrinth sits on the hilltop near Main Parking" (observed: 4qf.org camp tour).
- The flat 70 m strip between the creek and PA 26 on the west bank = New Camp's creek-side tent camping (fly-over
  t=219-255 s shows tents in forest along a straight creek reach with a gravel road).
- Creek in the gorge at 220 m matches USGS spot heights: 757 ft Silver Mills bridge (NE inflow), 743 ft at the south
  elbow, 720 ft on the west arm, 709 ft at the PA 484 bridge (observed: USGS EPQS).

## 1. Geography and scale
- Farm building "Four Quarters Interfaith Sanctuary, 190 Walker Lane" at 39.7450, -78.3688 (observed: OSM way
  1188413181; 4qf.org Google link 39.744896, -78.368804). Wikipedia's Artemas coordinates are the village 6 km west:
  wrong for the farm. New Camp entrance 39.745555, -78.372968 on PA 26 (observed: bigdubfestival.com/gateinfo).
- Acreage: 250 (4qf.org home), 150 (older 4qf land page), "over 200" (Big Dub Guide 2026 p.7), 300
  (bigdubfestival.com). All observed; the land grew by purchase (New Camp bought 2015 per GoFundMe).
- Creek course (observed: OSM way 934735891 + imagery; flows N to S): enters NE under the Silver Mills Rd bridge
  (39.7559, -78.3637), west along the north edge, hairpin at North Crook (~39.7560, -78.3700), south down a middle arm
  to a south-pointing elbow at 39.7511, -78.3688, WNW to an apex at 39.7541, -78.3774 (almost touching PA 26), south as
  the west arm (lon -78.371), then east along the south edge past the farmhouse to the PA 26 and PA 484 bridges
  (39.7431, -78.3592). Loop extent 1.45 km N-S x 1.3 km E-W. "Over a mile and a half of Sideling Creek" (observed:
  4qf.org). OSM's creek trace is hand-drawn and up to ~150 m off along the north edge; trust imagery and the DEM.
- "An enclosed river valley, bisected by Sideling Hill Creek... creek-side pools to dry cliff face, mature forest to
  hilltop meadow" (observed: Big Dub Guide 2026 p.7). "Horseshoe bend as it cuts through Town Hill Mountain" (observed:
  4qf.org land page); OSM's Town Hill ridge line is broken exactly here.
- Creek width: not stated anywhere. Pools 25-30 m wide at the Hemlock Hole, riffles ~15-20 m (inferred from imagery
  with scale bars and swim photos). American Whitewater lists the creek as Class I-II.
- Spacing (derived from the coordinates below): Stone Circle to Bridge ~300 m; Stone Circle to Camp Central buildings
  ~260 m; Stone Circle to Labyrinth ~430 m; Stone Circle to Farmhouse ~950 m; Bridge to Labyrinth ~210 m; Farmhouse
  to Silver Mills Rd junction 564 m; Farmhouse to New Camp entrance 365 m.

## 2. Stone Circle
- Count and size: "over 60 Standing Stones ... outlining almost two-thirds of the final 200-foot diameter circle"
  (observed: Big Dub Guide 2026 p.7). Design is an ellipse 200 ft E-W by 160 ft N-S, laid out on true north in 1995 and
  then rotated 12 degrees west of north to fit the land (observed:
  4qf.org/values/stone-circle/a-short-history-of-the-standing-stones). Older counts: 39 (4qf land page), 46
  (Roadside America, 2014), 53rd stone = the Flame Stone in 2017.
- Stones: sandstone from M and S Quarries in far western Maryland; typical slab 10,000-14,000 lb, up to 14 ft tall,
  8 ft wide, more than a foot thick (observed: 4qf working-the-stones, sizing-the-stones). Flame Stone: 22 ft, 4 t,
  red/brown/grey, set in the north as the first stone of a second interior ring (observed: naturalisticpaganism.org
  2017). Mother Stone: 13 ft reddish slab with an 18-inch drilled portal. West Gate: a pair that took two years to set,
  leaving a 20 ft gap. Each stone sits in a concrete footer below frost line (observed: 4qf history page). The North
  Copse: a 45 x 30 ft ellipse of ~12 ft beige sandstone slabs set almost touching, under construction 2019-2025
  (observed: 4qf.org/stonesrising).
- Ground and setting: "a forested hilltop"; the circle floor is bare packed dirt under tall straight hardwoods, with
  log benches and a North Altar (a draped flat stone slab table) inside; a dirt path runs along the stones (observed:
  stone_circle_row_of_slabs_dirt_path_tall_trees_earthskills.jpg, hiddenswimmingholes_grounds_1.jpg,
  stone_circle_north_altar_4qf.jpg). No central fire pit is documented. Big Dub's largest stage stands inside the
  circle; hammocks hang in the trees at its edge (observed: thesetlistud.com 2025; 4qf 2019 recap snippet).
- Location: inferred 39.7535, -78.3675, plus or minus 120 m, in the woods on the east bank of the middle creek arm,
  between the Camp Central buildings and the south elbow, downhill of the High Meadow (1998 stones were rolled "down the
  High Meadow into the circle"). Not visible in imagery (under canopy).

## 3. The Pavilion
- 4qf's "New Dance and Dining Pavilion", built 2010: 70 x 30 ft shell with a sprung dance floor, "perched on the
  hillside above Sidling Creek"; plans included a food service line (observed: 4qf.org/99-the-campground/camp-tour).
- Construction seen in photos: heavy timber post-and-beam frame with arched knee braces, open sides, green
  standing-seam metal gable roof, wooden deck floor on concrete piers, perimeter rail, long tables (observed:
  dining_pavilion_timber_frame_green_roof_deck_earthskills.jpg, pavilion_trusses_going_up_4qf.jpg,
  pavilion_interior_night_timber_posts_bigdub2023.jpg). Big Dub's Pavilion Stage and the HiScore game lounge are in it
  (observed: 2026 map legend).
- Other permanent structures (observed: same camp tour page): Starvin' Artist Cafe commercial kitchen (served 800 at
  one meal); Old Kitchen Pavilion (covered); a 24 x 24 ft stage with 10 ft to the rafters; Elder dormitory 2009 (double
  tongue-and-groove plank walls, 16 bunks, porch); Ceremonial Shed; Upper Shower House (high open framing, low-flush
  toilets, on-demand heaters); Lower Shower House 2005 (vine-covered, four showers on a shared deck plus two private
  stalls); Coffee Dragons' Lair (shaded deck beside the Upper Shower House); Drum Arbor = 85 ft sanded circle with a
  75 ft outer timber ring and a 46 ft open centre.

## 4. Hemlock Hole
- One of six swimming holes on the creek; "spectacular swimming area, shadowed by our cliffs" (observed:
  4qf.org/drumandsplash). Rules: no jumping, do not exit on the far bank, no soap (observed: Big Dub Guide 2026).
- Appearance: 25-30 m wide tea-coloured pool, hemlock boughs overhanging, a shale cliff on the outside of the bend, a
  gravel/shale beach on the inside where the crowd gathers (observed: hemlock_hole_* files; fly-over t=138-156 s).
- Stage placement: 2016 stages were Stone Circle, Pavilion, Hemlock Hole, Hilltop (observed: last.fm Big Dub 2016
  listing). 2019 map: "Pirate Stage & Hemlock Swimming Hole", with the Old Forest Stage across the bridge (observed:
  2019 map PDF text). 2026: the Hemlock Hole Stage sits on the bank above the pool with the Rainbow Bridge immediately
  beside it; the fly-over shows the bridge within ~50 m of the swimmers.
- Location: the hemlock-lined pool at the creek's south elbow, 39.7511, -78.3688, plus or minus 60 m (inferred: the
  4qf 2024 map puts it at the SW tip of the Camp Central loop; a dense hemlock band is visible there in leaf-off
  imagery; the Big Dub map agrees).

## 5. Rainbow Bridge
- History: built fall 2018 to summer 2019 on a bank loan so it would open for WickerMan and Big Dub 2019; reason:
  stepping-stone crossings had become reliably underwater; benefits listed: "a stunning walk through the tree tops",
  easy access to the Old Forest Stage, New Camp to Coffee Dragons walk "no further than a walk to Main Parking", fewer
  shuttles (observed: GoFundMe "Rainbow Bridge" by ProperOps LLC, created 2019-05-17, Wayback snapshot 2023-02-01).
- Type: permanent pedestrian suspension footbridge: steel main cables and hangers, wood-plank deck about 1.5 m wide,
  cable/chain-link mesh side panels, deck sagging slightly toward mid-span, spanning the pool roughly 8-10 m above the
  water (observed: rainbow_bridge_deck_view_night_earthskills.jpg,
  hemlock_hole_with_rainbow_bridge_above_swimmers_bigdub_a.jpg, yt_thumb_Qjt8ILxGfGo.jpg, fly-over frames
  t=159-171 s). Length not stated; about 60-70 m judging from the top-down drone frame (inferred). No LED lighting
  documented; origin of the name not found.
- Location: at/just downstream of the south elbow, about 39.7512, -78.3695, plus or minus 60 m (inferred). Which bank
  each end lands on could not be resolved in imagery (deck too narrow); the Big Dub map has its far end at New Camp
  Stage with the Old Forest to the south-west.

## 6. Roads, camps, vegetation, buildings
Anchor points (observed): farmhouse / Camp Office 39.7450, -78.3688 (OSM-tagged "190 Walker Lane"; green-roofed
cluster visible at 39.7452, -78.3690); Labyrinth rings 39.7497, -78.3681 (visible in leaf-off imagery); Camp Central
building cluster 39.7556, -78.3689 (buildings visible at the creek hairpin; identity inferred from 4qf text and maps);
New Camp entrance 39.745555, -78.372968 (bigdubfestival.com/gateinfo); Silver Mills Rd / Walker Lane junction
39.7450, -78.3622; Silver Mills Rd bridge 39.7559, -78.3637; PA 484 bridge 39.7431, -78.3592; creek apex by PA 26
39.7541, -78.3774.

Named areas (observed names: 4qf Camp Map 2024, Big Dub 2026 map, bigdubfestival.com/camp-services, 4qf camp tour;
coordinates inferred by matching the maps to imagery):
- Camp Central: service cluster (Coffee Dragons cafe/info/ice/firewood/shower tokens, Upper Shower House, main food
  vendors, EMS on the "Seminar Stage", merch, shuttle stop) at the NW corner of Main Camp beside the creek hairpin,
  39.7556, -78.3689. Coffee Dragons is "in the heart of camp by the Upper Shower House", also called a pavilion "on the
  High Meadow".
- North Crook: the hairpin bend ~39.7560, -78.3700: cool riverside forest camping with its own swimming hole, the Lower
  Shower House "at the base of the hill from the Pavilion, next to North Crook camping", shuttle stop, in-camp parking
  rows along the creek.
- Pavilion: on the hillside above the creek between Camp Central and North Crook, ~39.7552, -78.3692 (inferred).
- High Meadow: open scrub meadow with scattered small conifers NE of Camp Central, 39.752-39.7535, -78.3665 to
  -78.364, 800-840 ft; "on the right side of Ridge Road past RV Land".
- Big Bottom: flat creek-bottom land on the east bank of the middle arm below Camp Central, next to Members Camp (2024
  map), ~39.753, -78.368.
- Main Camp tent camping: forested slopes between Camp Central and the south elbow inside Loop Road, ~39.752, -78.367.
- White Pines: south end of Main Camp at the elbow (White Pines Loop road, ice, EMS, shuttle stop; conifers there in
  imagery), ~39.7515, -78.3685; Fox Altar beside it.
- Hilltop Camp / Main Parking / RV Land: the big mown pasture rising north from the farmhouse, 39.7470-39.7505,
  -78.3700 to -78.3665, 820 ft (south) to ~890 ft (north); gravel road up its west side with a hairpin at the NW corner;
  service structures and a row of stalls/hookups at its north edge 39.7508, -78.3690; Ridge Road along its north; RV
  camping on Ridge Road near Hilltop (2026 map); Labyrinth at its north end 39.7497, -78.3681.
- New Camp: tent, car and RV camping between PA 26 and the creek's west arm, 39.745-39.751, -78.374 to -78.371; own
  gate on PA 26; New Camp Loop road; a small mown field 39.7465-39.7485, -78.3745 to -78.3715; the flat 70 m creek-side
  strip is its tent camping.
- The Old Forest: conifer-heavy forest between New Camp and the creek bends, ~39.752, -78.373 (Old Forest Stage, tent
  camping).
- Fairview Station: parking field west of PA 26 opposite the New Camp entrance, ~39.746, -78.375 (fly-over shows a huge
  lot with a green-roofed barn).
- Camp Offices / Main Registration / Main Entrance: the farmhouse cluster, reached by Walker Lane from Silver Mills
  Road (564 m to the east). Loft & Shop buildings ~39.7478, -78.3666 on the road up the ridge. Members Camp: wooden tent
  platforms on a forested hillside (no coordinates found).

Roads: "more than a mile and a half of graveled roads"; Ridge Road "runs from Main Parking past RV Land, straightens
into the heart of camp, turns sharply right, and slopes gently downhill toward the river" (observed: camp tour). Walker
Lane = private E-W gravel road at lat 39.745 (OSM way 19382131); "Bridgeport Road" (OSM 19592539) climbs from the
farmhouse NE up the ridge; a gravel road climbs the west side of the Hilltop field (OSM 420928422); Upper/Lower
Switchback descend from the PA 26 side; Loop Road, River Road, Hill Top Trail, Farm House Road are 2024-map names.

Vegetation (observed: camp tour, pittsburghquarterly.com, imagery): mixed Appalachian hardwoods (tall straight
oak/tulip trunks over the circle), eastern hemlock along the creek and at the Hemlock Hole, white pine copses along the
farm road and at White Pines, mown hay on the Hilltop and farm fields, scrub meadows with small conifers, shale cliffs
with shale-barren plants on the outsides of bends; dark conifer bands follow the creek in leaf-off imagery; orchards and
100+ planted trees round the farmhouse.

Labyrinth: modified Cretan 7-circuit, 76 ft diameter (older page 84 ft, 264 ft around), path almost half a mile,
granite curb blocks sunk in the ground, unfinished circuits mown into grass, solar lights, four tall wooden solstice
poles on the rim, ~50% complete April 2024 (observed: 4qf.org/values/stone-circle/the-labyrinth-at-four-quarters).

Fire Circle = Upper Fire & Drum Circle, 85 ft sand ring with timber arbor; Big Dub runs flame jets round it. Sweat
Lodge by the river: Welcome Fire, low tarp dome ~2.5 m, stones heated in a fire pit. Fairy Cairn: knee-high stone cairn
hung with baubles and lights on the riverbank. Fox, Wolf, Oak Spring, Warriors altars: slab tables by the creek (all
observed: 4qf pages, 2019/2024 maps, photos).

Homestead: farmhouse c.1890, south-facing, wood-heated, grapevine porch; Loft & Shop 2007, 3,500 sq ft, earth-bermed;
winery; Visitors House 1/3 mile away (observed: 4qf farmhouse-tour, visitors-house pages).
