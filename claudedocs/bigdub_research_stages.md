# Big Dub Festival - stages, production and festival facts (research report, 2026-10-09)

Scope: Big Dub Festival, Four Quarters Interfaith Sanctuary, 190 Walker Lane, Artemas PA. Grounds were researched separately; this report covers stages, production, branding and festival facts. Labels: (observed: source) = read/seen directly; (inferred) = my reading of photos/frames; "not found" = searched, nothing located. Filenames refer to images in this folder; see manifest.md. Frame IDs: QnjApblE-fw = 2026 aftermovie, aqs-2mkpmYA = 2025 recap, N3GmCSv372w = 2023 recap, 8ScW4DYbX9k = 2024 recap; _tNNN = seconds into the video.

## 2 Stage design per stage per year

### Stone Circle Stage (main stage, inside the standing-stone circle)
Site facts: the circle "began in 1995"; "over 60 Standing Stones ... outlining almost two-thirds of the final 200-foot diameter circle" (observed: 2026 guide p7). Standing stones are visible at the crowd edges in N3GmCSv372w_t018 (empty, day), QnjApblE-fw_t120 (night crowd) and at both sides of the stage in N3GmCSv372w_t030.
- 2023 (observed: N3GmCSv372w_t030 day wide, _t042 and _t129 night, _t132 DJ view): raised deck under a flat box-truss roof (front truss with moving heads and blinders); centre LED wall behind the DJ booth; one portrait LED panel each side hung in front of the trees; a tall painted/printed facade of psychedelic knotwork serpent/dragon artwork in orange-pink-purple flanking the whole stage, with a silver sun/gear emblem on blue fabric centred on top of the truss; flown line-array each side plus ground subs; crowd barricade; red/pink printed stretch-fabric shade sails strung overhead; dirt floor with live trees inside the audience area; wooden benches. Night: red laser fans over the crowd (_t042), green laser arcs (_t057).
- 2024 (observed: site/BIGDUB24_GRAYSONHALLPHOTO-588.jpg night; 8ScW4DYbX9k_t135 day from stage): centre LED wall showing red/white spiral graphics; four portrait LED pillars (two per side) also playing spiral content; truss roof with moving heads; purple wash; green laser fans launched from above/behind the stage across the crowd; trees standing in the crowd. Painted facade not visible in the night photo (unknown whether used in 2024).
- 2025 (observed: aqs-2mkpmYA_t120 day wide, _t114, _t123 night, _t135 night, _t039): box-truss roof with moving heads, blinders and strobes; centre LED wall; two tall portrait LED panels on scaffold towers flanking the stage; a second pair of LED panels further out among the trees on both sides; black PA stacks each side; low deck with DJ booth; night: purple wash, white laser fans, white spiky "flower" inflatables hung in the trees (_t135); flame-effect fireballs (_t138, location on site not confirmed).
- 2026 (observed: QnjApblE-fw_t147 night wide, _t042 day crowd, _t051, _t057, _t120): centre LED wall (reads about three panels wide), two tall LED pillars each side near the stage, plus at least three more pillars per side out in the crowd; a glowing sun/flower emblem with rays above the wall (inferred: same emblem piece as the 2023 silver sun, now lit); laser fans from both sides crossing over the crowd (cyan/white at _t147, red+purple at _t051, green at _t057); blue inflatable jellyfish and white spiky flower sculptures hung in the trees over the crowd (_t042); heavy haze; purple wash elsewhere.
- Close-up detail, year not stated (observed: site/DAY2_BIGDUB_MAKOPHOTOOFFICIAL-91.jpg, on the current homepage so 2025 or 2026 inferred): LED wall spans the full width behind the booth; the DJ booth front is itself an LED panel; top truss carries about eight moving heads plus LED batten/strobe bars; side towers carry LED panels; deck roughly chest-to-head height above the crowd (inferred); PA stack visible at left.
- Dimensions: NOT FOUND in any source. Use proportions from the frames only.

### Pavilion Stage / "HiScore" (inside the timber-frame pavilion)
- Set times call it HiSCORE (2025, 2026); activities say "HiScore Pavilion", "Hiscore at Pavilion Stage", "Field next to HiScore Pavilion" (2026) and "Hill Next to HiScore Pavilion" (2025) (observed: program PDFs). "Hi-Score ... a fully decorated video game lounge ... over 300 games with 18 consoles", bracketed tournaments, cosplay pageants (observed: bigdubfestival.com/hi-score/). First stage open, from Tuesday (2026 guide p8).
- Decor 2023-2026 (observed: site/hiscoreq7.jpg, N3GmCSv372w_t090, 8ScW4DYbX9k_t018, aqs-2mkpmYA_t063, QnjApblE-fw_t036): exposed timber frame with arched braces; long strands of artificial wisteria/flower garlands hanging from every rafter; red/pink/purple uplighting; rows of gaming tables with monitors and consoles down the middle; Mario-universe character cutouts (Toad, Shy Guy) in the older photo, purple cartoon astronaut/raver cutouts in 2026; DJ booth at the far end under the arches, booth sides dressed with ornate carved-look panels in 2024; flat screens on the walls; a Shy Guy mask displayed inside a round portal cutout outside (8ScW4DYbX9k_t006). Night exterior 2023: glowing paper-lantern "planet" spheres (pink, orange, cyan) hung in the trees beside the pavilion (N3GmCSv372w_t078).

### Hemlock Hole Stage (bank above the swimming hole, by the Rainbow Bridge)
- Named HEMLOCK HOLE in 2025/2026 set times; opens Wednesday with the "SWEET SOUNDS OPENING PARTY"; hosts the Twerk Contest, DJ Battle, Pillo Talk Pillow Party (observed: 2026 guide pp8-11).
- Design: no frame is captioned with the stage name. Candidate (inferred): the small ground-support "goal-post" truss stage with sea-creature decor seen in 2024 (8ScW4DYbX9k_t108, _t129) and 2025 (aqs-2mkpmYA_t060): two truss towers and a top truss with moving heads; a purple-pink inflatable octopus sitting on top of the truss; inflatable jellyfish (pink, yellow, green, cyan) hung in the surrounding trees; a green neon dinosaur figure on a tower; a small canopy tent beside it; barricade in front. The swimming-hole location makes the under-the-sea theme plausible but it is NOT confirmed; it could equally be New Camp.

### New Camp Stage (across the Rainbow Bridge, Old Forest side)
- Named NEW CAMP in set times; daytime workshops (Big Dub 101, DJ etiquette, Intro to Ableton, Open Decks hosted by Scratch Camp), nightly DJs, "Scratch Camp Block Party" 1 AM Friday (observed: 2026 guide pp8-10). Ambient Alchemists takeover on the "New Camp Stage" 27 Jul 2024 (observed: ambientalchemists.com). Design: no identified photo. Candidate: 8ScW4DYbX9k_t036 (night, goal-post truss, white beams) - unconfirmed.

- Mushroomland: giant inflatable red-capped white-spotted mushrooms (about twice human height, inferred) and pink/purple inflatable flowers, with the mascot in the middle (N3GmCSv372w_t111 2023, 8ScW4DYbX9k_t063 2024, QnjApblE-fw_t153 2026).

## 4 Festival facts
- Attendance: no official figure. Seen: "around 4,000 people every year" (ticketsmarter snippet), "5,000 attendees" in 2017 with "a cut in capacity by nearly 50%" (raverrafting 2017 snippet; page not fetchable), "5,000-15,000" (frontstagefestivals listing estimate), "the cube" secret-set room "capacity is like 60" (Daily Frequency 2025). Treat all as unofficial.
- Mascot: KIKIWAWA (spelling from the on-screen caption "Get well soon Kikiwawa, the storm may have taken you, but not your spirit", aqs-2mkpmYA_t162). A yellow egg/pear-shaped inflatable with black double-spiral eyes, a wide open smiling mouth with pink tongue, two stubby arms, two feet and a tuft of green leaves on top. Seen 2023 (N3GmCSv372w_t015, _t039), 2024 (8ScW4DYbX9k_t063, among mushrooms), 2025 (in the camp meadow next to the "Big Dub NEWS" reporter, _t003; damaged by the final-day 2025 thunderstorm), 2026 (the aftermovie plot is the reporter searching for Kikiwawa; found among inflatable mushrooms and flowers, QnjApblE-fw_t153; by tents _t159). Height roughly two to three people (inferred from _t153). Cartoon Kikiwawas appear on the Lil Dub 2026 poster (guide p35). The name was not found in any text source.
- Logo/branding: "BIG DUB" in ornate swirling psychedelic lettering with "FESTIVAL" in small caps beneath; spirals inside the letters and a spiral finial on top; the 2026 cover frames it with red-capped white-spotted mushrooms and daisies (observed: guide p1, site/Big-Dub-Horizontal.png, aftermovie end card). The spiral motif is reused as 2024 LED content. The mushroom motif dates to "Mushroomland" 2014.
- Next dates: Big Dub 2027 July 20-25 (aftermovie end card); Lil Dub "Big Dub Re-Union" Sept 25-27 2026, lildubfest.com (guide p35).
- Art installations named: Illuminarium (UV lasers + blacklight glow objects), Camp Portal gallery ("Cipofjoelle" 12 Step series, RGB colour-change portal), murals/live painting (site), LED hoops, lantern planets, inflatable sea creatures, Mushroomland. Artists for 2023-26 beyond Cipofjoelle: not found.
- Food vendors 2025 (observed: bigdubfestival.com/food2023/, titled "Big Dub 2025 Food Vendor Menus"): Wok N Tok (Asian), Naders Bistro (festival fare), Sassy Wraps, Puttin on the Mitts (pastries, sandwiches, desserts, drinks); plus the Four Quarters Coffee Dragons Cafe at Camp Central (ice, WiFi vouchers). 2026 list: not found (the guide has no vendor page).
- Theme camps 2025 (observed: program p12): Lucid Lounge, Camp Disco Circus, Camp Flow With Us, The Stoop, The Illuminarium, Scratch Camp, The Sauce Sanctuary, The Expanse, The Disco Den, Flow Therapy for Mind and Body (at Hilltop), Frick Frack, Follow The White Rabbit, Camp Clown-A-Round; activity locations also Tea Table at Hammock Haven, Goddess Krownz vending booth, Riverside Sweat Lodge Camp, Lucid District.
- Theme camps 2026 (observed: guide pp12-13): Lucid Lounge, Camp Disco Circus, Camp Flow With Us, The Stoop, The Illuminarium, Scratch Camp, The Sauce Sanctuary, The Expanse, The Disco Den, Flow Therapy for Mind and Body, Frick Frack, Clown Town! ("Bonkers for Honkers"), Follow The White Rabbit, Camp Clown-A-Round, gRaveYard Smash (Halloween), Camp Portal, Camp Truffle (EZBreezy Eats).
