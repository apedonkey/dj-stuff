# Crowd V2: licenses

Everything the crowd uses is made by `crowd2.py` itself. No third-party models, textures, animations or code are in
it, so nothing here needs attribution or a license beyond the project's own:

- **People:** lofted from parameters in `crowd2.py`. This covers bodies, heads, hair styles, caps, bucket hats,
  beanies, hoods, backpacks, phones, glowsticks, poles, flags, LED signs, inflatables and dino cut-outs.
- **Rig and dance clips:** procedural poses written in `crowd2.py`.
- **Colours:** palettes in `crowd2.py`.
- **Vertex animation texture:** baked from the above by `crowd2.py`.

CC0 packs such as Quaternius's characters were considered and not used. Procedural people let one script control the
tri budget, the shared rig and the vertex-animation layout, and they need no downloads or logins.
